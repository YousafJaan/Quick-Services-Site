# Quick Services Academy — Content Standards & Editorial Style Guide

## 1. Editorial Voice & Reading Level

### 1.1 Target Audience
Our primary learners are university students in Pakistan (enrolled in BS, MS, MPhil, and PhD programmes) and researchers in similar developing higher education contexts. Many are writing high-stakes academic dissertations in English as a second language (ESL/EFL).

### 1.2 Core Principles
1. **Clarity Over Ornamentation**: Write in plain, direct English. Every sentence must communicate one unambiguous idea.
2. **Action-Oriented Verbs**: Use strong, concrete verbs. Minimise passive voice unless describing experimental procedure where the actor is intentionally omitted.
3. **Spelling & Conventions**: Use standard British English spelling (`programme`, `analyse`, `behaviour`, `modelling`, `summarise`), matching the academic conventions of Pakistani and Commonwealth universities.
4. **Tone**: Direct, encouraging, authoritative, and respectful. Address the learner directly as "you". Never adopt an elitist or condescending tone.

### 1.3 Stylistic Prohibitions
To preserve readability and ensure high pedagogical value across all devices:
- **No Rhetorical Questions as Openers**: Do not start paragraphs with "Have you ever wondered...?" or "What makes a good thesis?". State the core principle directly.
- **No Mannered Framing**: Avoid "not X, but Y" constructions, excessive dash-led asides, and dramatic colon-then-reveal sentences.
- **No Cliché Academic Filler**: Prohibit phrases like "In today's fast-paced digital era...", "It is interesting to note that...", "Needless to say...", or "A plethora of studies...".

## 2. Sentence Architecture & Visual Pairing

Because **every sentence of teaching text receives its own pre-made visual component**, sentence construction must follow strict constraints:
- **One Idea Per Sentence**: Each sentence must express a single verifiable relationship, sequence, definition, or comparative rule.
- **Sentence Length**: Target 12 to 22 words per sentence. Split compound sentences into two discrete sentences whenever possible.
- **Addressability**: Every sentence is tagged with a unique data attribute (e.g., `<span data-s="thesis101-m1-l1-p2-s3">`) to allow instant toolbar mapping.

---

## 3. Strict Academic Integrity Rules

Quick Services Academy operates under a zero-tolerance policy regarding academic dishonesty:
1. **Teaching Over Ghostwriting**: We teach research methodologies, statistical analysis, and editorial principles. We never author text intended for direct student submission.
2. **No Evasion Techniques**: Never instruct students on how to fool similarity algorithms (Turnitin, iThenticate), circumvent AI detectors, or deceive viva examiners. We teach ethical paraphrasing, rigorous citation, and honest disclosure.
3. **Clear Labelling of Invented Examples**: All mock research datasets, survey results, author citations, and sample questionnaires must be explicitly marked: *"Note: This dataset is fictional and created solely for instructional purposes."*
4. **Official Policy Citations**: All regulatory policies (e.g., HEC Pakistan Plagiarism Policy 2020) must be cited with their official titles and institutional clauses. Never invent policy thresholds.

---

## 4. Assessment & Quiz Writing Guidelines

1. **Realistic Distractors**: Distractors must reflect authentic conceptual errors made by research students (e.g., confusing sample size $N$ with statistical power $1-\beta$, or confusing a moderator with a mediator).
2. **Prohibited Options**: Never use "All of the above", "None of the above", or "Both A and B". Each option must be a standalone proposition.
3. **Randomized Positions**: The correct answer must be evenly distributed across all option positions.
4. **Actionable Explanations**: Every question—whether answered correctly or incorrectly—must display an explanation that explains *why* the correct answer is valid and *why* the distractors fail, accompanied by a link back to the specific lesson sentence.
