# Quick Services Academy — Primary Sources & Regulatory References

This ledger documents the official primary sources, documentation, and regulatory policies consulted for every course and factual claim across the academy.

---

## 1. Regulatory & Institutional Standards

1. **Higher Education Commission of Pakistan (HEC)**:
   - *HEC Plagiarism Policy (2020)*: Official guidelines establishing the 19% cumulative similarity threshold and 5% single-source limit on Turnitin reports, defining permissible exclusions for quotes, bibliography, and standardized methodological descriptions.
   - *HEC Journal Recognition System (HJRS)*: Official classification categories (W, X, Y) for evaluating peer-reviewed journals published nationally and internationally.
   - *HEC Guidelines for MS/MPhil and PhD Theses Examination*: Prescribed defense protocols, external evaluator requirements, and minimum publication criteria.

## 2. Statistical Analysis & Methodology References

1. **Statistical Power & Sample Size Determination**:
   - Faul, F., Erdfelder, E., Lang, A.-G., & Buchner, A. (2007). *G*Power 3: A flexible statistical power analysis program for the social, behavioral, and biomedical sciences*. Behavior Research Methods, 39(2), 175–191.
   - Cochran, W. G. (1977). *Sampling Techniques* (3rd ed.). John Wiley & Sons. (Formulas for unknown populations and finite population correction).
   - Cohen, J. (1988). *Statistical Power Analysis for the Behavioral Sciences* (2nd ed.). Lawrence Erlbaum Associates. (Standard effect size benchmarks for small, medium, and large effects across $d, f, r, f^2$).

2. **Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)**:
   - Hair, J. F., Hult, G. T. M., Ringle, C. M., & Sarstedt, M. (2022). *A Primer on Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM)* (3rd ed.). SAGE Publications. (Official thresholds: Outer loadings $\ge 0.708$, AVE $\ge 0.50$, CR $\ge 0.70$, $VIF < 3.3 / 5.0$, Bootstrapping 5,000 samples).
   - Henseler, J., Ringle, C. M., & Sarstedt, M. (2015). *A new criterion for assessing discriminant validity in variance-based structural equation modeling*. Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, 43(1), 115–135. (HTMT criterion thresholds: $HTMT < 0.85$ conservative, $HTMT < 0.90$ liberal).

3. **SPSS Guidelines & General Statistics**:
   - Field, A. (2018). *Discovering Statistics Using IBM SPSS Statistics* (5th ed.). SAGE Publications. (Assumptions for normality, Levene's test, post-hoc selection, multicollinearity diagnostics).
   - Pallant, J. (2020). *SPSS Survival Manual: A Step by Step Guide to Data Analysis Using IBM SPSS* (7th ed.). McGraw-Hill Education.
