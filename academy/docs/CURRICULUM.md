## Learning Paths (`data/paths.json`)

### 1. Thesis Starter Path (`path-thesis`)
- **Target Audience**: Students beginning their BS, MS, MPhil, or PhD proposals/theses.
- **Sequence**:
  1. `thesis101` — Thesis Writing 101
  2. `cite101` — Citations and Referencing
  3. `integrity101` — Academic Integrity & Similarity Reports
  4. `edit101` — Proofreading & Academic Editing
  5. `viva` — Viva & Presentation Coaching

### 2. Research & Statistics Path (`path-stats`)
- **Target Audience**: Researchers conducting quantitative empirical studies and data analysis.
- **Sequence**:
  1. `lit-search` — Literature Search with Zotero
  2. `sample-size` — Sample Size & Research Methodology
  3. `spss101` — SPSS Basics for Research Students
  4. `smartpls` — SmartPLS & SEM Introduction

## Detailed Course Breakdowns

### Course 1: Thesis Writing 101 (`thesis101`)
*Tagline: From vague idea to an approved, defensible thesis proposal and manuscript.*
- **Outcomes**:
  - Narrow broad topics into focused, researchable questions using the FINER framework.
  - Structure an empirical thesis across five core chapters with proportional word budgets.
  - Synthesize literature thematically rather than producing an annotated bibliography.
  - Communicate effectively and professionally with academic supervisors.
- **Modules**:
  - **Module 1: Finding Your Question & The Proposal**
    - `t1` (read, 10 min): What Makes a Thesis Topic Work? (Pilot Lesson)
    - `t2` (visual, 12 min): The Topic Narrowing Funnel [Widget: `funnel`]
    - `t3` (video, 8 min): Video: Crafting Defensible Problem Statements [Video: `V-THESIS-01`]
    - `t4` (read, 11 min): Writing the Research Proposal Step-by-Step
    - `t5` (quiz, 10 min): Module 1 Assessment: Topic & Proposal Mastery
  - **Module 2: Chapter Architecture & Literature Review**
    - `t6` (visual, 10 min): Thesis Word Budget & Chapter Planner [Widget: `planner`]
    - `t7` (read, 12 min): The Synthesis Matrix: Connecting Studies Thematically
    - `t8` (video, 7 min): Video: Avoiding the Annotated Bibliography Trap [Video: `V-THESIS-02`]
    - `t9` (read, 11 min): Writing the Literature Review Chapter
    - `t10` (quiz, 10 min): Module 2 Assessment: Literature Review Synthesis
  - **Module 3: Methodology, Results & Discussion**
    - `t11` (read, 12 min): Aligning Research Questions with Methodology
    - `t12` (video, 8 min): Video: Reporting Quantitative & Qualitative Findings [Video: `V-THESIS-03`]
    - `t13` (read, 12 min): Writing the Discussion: Interpreting vs Repeating Results

  - **Module 4: Introduction, Conclusion & Three-Pass Editing**
