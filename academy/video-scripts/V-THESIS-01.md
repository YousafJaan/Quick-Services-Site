---

## 1. Learning Objectives
1. Understand the difference between an academic topic, a practical dilemma, and an empirical research problem.
2. Master the Three-Part Problem Statement Formula: The Ideal State (Hook), The Reality Gap (Conflict), and The Theoretical/Practical Consequence (Significance).
3. Identify and eliminate the common "lack of research" fallacy in student proposals.
