FAQ

Common questions

Will you write my thesis or assignment for me?

No. We coach, edit your own writing, and explain the statistical methods. The work submitted to your university must be authored by you.

Do you help reduce Turnitin or AI-detection scores?

No. We teach you to paraphrase honestly and cite primary literature correctly, which is the only legitimate and lasting way to produce a clean similarity report.

How quickly can you help?

It depends on scope. Standard turnaround is 5–7 days; express 48–72 hour support is available for urgent deadlines. Message M. Yousaf on WhatsApp to check availability.

Is my data kept private?

Yes. All draft documents, questionnaires, and raw datasets are handled with strict institutional confidentiality and never shared with third parties.

What payment methods do you accept?

JazzCash, EasyPaisa, SadaPay, NayaPay, local bank transfer, or Western Union for international scholars.

Which tools and citation styles do you support?

SPSS and SmartPLS for statistics; APA 7th, Harvard, Vancouver, and IEEE for citation formatting. Mention your university guidelines when contacting us.
